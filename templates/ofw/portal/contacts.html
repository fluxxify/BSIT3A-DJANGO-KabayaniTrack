What to do in an emergency

  • Call your country's embassy or POLO office first for legal assistance.
  • Call OWWA's 24/7 hotline 1348 (from abroad: +632 8551 1545).
  • Report serious incidents to the police, then notify the management team through your Concerns page.
  • Keep your family emergency contacts up to date so loved ones can be reached quickly.
